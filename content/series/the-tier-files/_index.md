The TIER system is Alberta's flagship climate policy. It puts a price on carbon for large industrial emitters and collects roughly $600 million per year into a dedicated fund. The fund is supposed to be reinvested into emissions-reducing technology. But the public filings tell a different story.

This series follows the money: where TIER revenue comes from, where it actually goes, and what happens to the gap in between.
