Lars J. Frank writes under a pen name. The work published here comes from public documents: audited financial statements, annual reports, regulatory filings, budget documents, and technical appendices. The subject is usually the gap between official narratives and the accounting that sits underneath them. No leaks. No inside sources. Just public records and arithmetic.
