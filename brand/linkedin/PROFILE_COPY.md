## About

Lars J. Frank writes under a pen name. The work at The Sense of Nonsense comes from public documents: audited financial statements, annual reports, regulatory filings, budget documents, and technical appendices. The subject is usually the gap between official narratives and the accounting that sits underneath them. No leaks. No inside sources. Just public records and arithmetic.

The TIER Files follows Alberta's industrial carbon money through the TIER Fund and Emissions Reduction Alberta. Across the series: transfers and accumulated surplus, grant commitments versus cash paid to projects, interest on held balances, environmental-attribute accounting across provincial systems, and related-party remuneration disclosed in audited notes.

Stories and analysis from within the nonsense.
